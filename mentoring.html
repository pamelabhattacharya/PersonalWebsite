Why I am doing this

All my life, people have helped me selflessly. Some were people who never knew me. Some knew me, and I may not always have done my best with them, but they helped me anyway. Those acts of generosity changed the course of my life in ways that are hard to fully measure.

This is one small way I want to pay that forward. I cannot promise that I will have every answer, but I can promise to show up with care, honesty, and a real effort to help you find a path through whatever you are carrying.

What we can discuss

This space is for people who are serious about growing, willing to reflect honestly, and looking for a thoughtful conversation rather than a shortcut.

Career and growth

Navigating roles, teams, leadership, technical direction, interviews, transitions, or moments when your next move feels unclear.

Work through a hard problem

Talking through a situation where you need a clear head, a second perspective, or help turning uncertainty into concrete options.

Finding the right person

If I am not the right person to help directly, I will try my best to identify someone in my network who may be able to support you.

How the session works

Each session is meant to be practical, human, and low-pressure. You do not need to arrive with a polished question. You can arrive with a messy situation and we will work from there.

I will treat our conversation as confidential. I will listen first, ask questions, share what I can from my own experience, and help you leave with a clearer next step.

If I feel I cannot directly help you, I will say that honestly and, where possible, try to connect you with people who might be better placed to help.

A good fit if you are...

  • At a career crossroads and unsure how to evaluate your options.
  • Trying to grow as an engineer, researcher, manager, or builder.
  • Facing a difficult work situation and needing a private sounding board.
  • Working on something ambitious and feeling blocked or alone in it.
  • Looking for honest perspective from someone outside your immediate circle.
  • Unsure who to ask for help, but ready to ask someone.

What this is not

To keep this space useful and respectful, I want to be clear about the boundaries. This is not a referral channel, job placement service, therapy session, fundraising pitch review, or free consulting engagement.

Not a shortcut

Please come with a real question, context, and willingness to think together. I can help you reason through next steps, but I cannot do the work for you.

Not a referral request

Please do not use this primarily to ask for referrals, introductions, or job opportunities. If a useful connection naturally emerges, I may offer it.

Not ongoing coaching

I am starting with two free 50-minute conversations each week. This is a focused conversation, not an ongoing coaching program.

Request a free session

I will review requests and choose two sessions each week based on fit, where I think I can be useful, and the kind of community I hope to build around generosity, seriousness, curiosity, and mutual respect.
